------------------------------------------------------------
 FEATURES BUILT SO FAR
------------------------------------------------------------

[1] Pencil Cursor
    - Custom pencil cursor that points up, tilted -30 degrees.
    - Hotspot tuned so the tip of the pencil is the click point.

[2] Square-Ruled Notebook Background
    - Whole app sits on a light square-ruled grid (like real paper)
      and the LOADING SCREEN matches the same white ruled style.

[3] Loading Screen - Diary Cover
    - A floating diary cover with brownish-red leather texture.
    - Gold spiral coils + punch holes on the LEFT side (removed right side).
    - Spinner + "By Your Husband with Love" message.
    - Fades away smoothly once the page is ready.

[4] Global Search
    - Search bar in the header with live dropdown results.
    - Jumps to the matching lesson/section.
    - Highlights the found text with a flash animation.

[5] Animated Cat Mascot (REALISTIC SVG CAT)
    - Real cat SVG (fur texture, amber eyes, whiskers, claws).
    - Appears from the right corner to say hi every hour:
      "hey your Husband loves you <3"
    - If you stay idle 10 minutes, it reminds you to study:
      "It's Time to Study My Love <3"
    - Cat's eyes follow your mouse.
    - Click the cat to make it say hi anytime.

[6] Focus Mode Button (emerald physical button)
    - Press to disable the cat interruptions and study in quiet.
    - Same squishy physical-button feel as the night light.

[7] Night Light Button (amber physical button)
    - Warm orange screen tint for night time.
    - Full-screen light sweep animation when toggled.
    - Golden glow on the button in dark mode.
    - Remembers your choice between visits (saved in localStorage).

[8] Cursor Trail
    - Dashed pencil-like trail follows the mouse around the page,
      drawn behind the content on a canvas.

[9] Interactivity / Polish
    - Active nav highlighting (button jiggles as you hover via
      mouse-position glow effect).
    - Tabs (Lesson 1-5, Scales, Calculators, Quiz, Review) switch
      without reloading the page.
    - Progress percentage updates as you complete topic sections.
    - Fireworks when you score a perfect quiz.

------------------------------------------------------------
 NOTES
------------------------------------------------------------
- Everything saved to browser (night light / focus mode) uses
  localStorage, so it survives page refreshes within the browser.
- No backend or database required; works 100% with GitHub Pages.
- Static files served by GitHub Pages: just push to main branch
  and the site updates automatically.
